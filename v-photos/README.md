## What you can see in the photos

**Front** — The Pi Camera Module 3 sits at the top on its 3D printed mount, looking forward over the front wheels. The front HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor is in the middle of the chassis, below the camera.

**Sides** — The side views show the two-level layout. The drive motor, steering servo and the Ackermann steering linkage are on the bottom plate, and the Raspberry Pi 5, the ESP32 and the wiring are on the top plate. The side ultrasonic sensor is mounted in the middle of the chassis.

**Top** — From above you can see the Raspberry Pi 5 in its case, the orange camera ribbon cable running forward to the camera, and the three 18650 Li-ion cells that power the vehicle.

**Rear** — The rear view shows the battery holder and the rear wheel drive.

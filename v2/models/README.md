# 3D Mechanical CAD Models (`/models`)

This directory contains the custom 3D Computer-Aided Design (CAD) models, structural assembly parts, bracket mounts, and wheel hubs developed for the **Team Yo_Error_404** autonomous competition vehicle.

All structural models are provided in **STL** format and printed in high-durability **PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol)** filament at **100% infill** to ensure high flexural fatigue resistance under motor acceleration and cornering loads.

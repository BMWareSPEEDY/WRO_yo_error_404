## What you can see in the photos

**Front** — The Pi Camera Module 3 sits at the top on its 3D printed mount, looking forward over the front wheels. The front HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor is in the middle of the chassis, below the camera.

**Sides** — The side views show the two-level layout. The drive motor, the steering servo and the Ackermann steering linkage are on the bottom plate, along with the ESP32 and the L298N motor driver. The Raspberry Pi 5 and the battery cells sit on the top plate, with the wiring running up through the gap between the two levels. The side ultrasonic sensor is mounted in the middle of the chassis rather than at the nose, so that its reading does not change when the front wheels turn.

**Top** — From above you can see the Raspberry Pi 5 in its case, the orange camera ribbon cable running forward to the camera, the 18650 Li-ion cells that power the vehicle, and the power switch.

**Rear** — The rear view shows the battery holder and the rear wheel drive.
